Importers and Exporters

	

The supplier has import and export rights

	

Does the company have a valid Import and Export license
该项值为Yes




High Repeat Buyers Choice

	

More than 50% of buyers repeatedly choose the supplier

	

Repeat Buyers Choice
该项值>=50%




Cooperated with Fortune 500

	

This supplier has Cooperated with Fortune 500 companies

	

Cooperated with Fortune 500
该项的值为：yes




Years of Export Experience

	

The export experience of the supplier is more than 10 years

	

Export experience
该项值>=10




Multi-language Pioneer

	

【贵司擅长的语种数量】 languages freely used by foreign trade staff. include:【贵司擅长的语种清单】

	

The language freely used by foreign trade staff
该项值包含的语种数量>=2（含英语）




Experienced Team

	

The supplier has 【贵司外贸员工数】 foreign trading staff(s) and 【贵司6年以上经验外贸员工数】 staff(s) with over 6 years of overseas trading experience

	

The number of foreign trading staff with relevant trading experience
1、外贸人员总数不少于5
2、经验>=6年的外贸人员不少于2




Self-branded

	

The supplier has 【贵司自有品牌数】 Self-brands, check the Audit Report for more information

	

Own brand name不为空
Own brand quantities > 0


或

Trademark information不为空








Patents Awarded

	

The supplier had awarded 【贵司获得专利数】 patents, you can check the Audit Report for more information

	

Patent Situation至少填写一组




Exhibition experience

	

The supplier had participated in offline trade shows, you can check the Audit Report for more information

	

Have you participated in offline trade shows?
该项的值为：yes




National Award received

	

This supplier has received national award, you can check the Audit Report for more information

	

Does the company receive a national award?
以下两项中，任意一项被勾选：
Internationally recognized design awards
National Science and Technology Progress Award

Overseas services countries

Quality Assurance

	

The supplier provides quality assurance

Raw-Materials Traceability

Is there traceability identification of raw materials?
该项的值为：yes




QA/QC Inspectors

	

The supplier has 【贵司QA及QC员工数】 QA and QC inspection staff.

	

Number of QA and QC inspection staff
该项值>=1




100% Finished Products Inspection

	

The supplier inspects 100% of finished products.

	

What type of inspection is used for finished products?
该项选择100% inspection




Standardized quality control

	

The supplier has a complete and standardized quality control process, check the Audit Report for more information

	

以下3个条件全部满足：
1、Instructions available for incoming material inspections /testing，relevant records maintained（选1）
2、necessary items /documents provided at appropriate location and under control（选1或2或3）
3、written inspections /testing instructions available for finished products（选1）

R&D Capabilities

	

The supplier has 【贵司研发工程师数】 R&D engineers, you can check the Audit Report for more information

	

1、There is/are __R＆D engineer(s) in the company
该项值大于等于1
2、What is the main job responsibility for R&D engineers？
该项值不为空







Production Process Visualization

	

You can check the supplier's production workflow chart or main facilities in the Audit Report

	

1、Number of production lines
此项不为空
2、Production Workflow Chart
此项不为空
3、Main Facilities
此项不为空

资质及认证（Qualifications and Certifications）

	

Management Certification

	

The supplier has quality management system certification, including:【贵司获得的管理认证清单】

	

Management system certification下至少有一个认证项（含Others）




Product Certification

	

The supplier's products already have relevant certification qualifications, including:【贵司获得的产品认证清单】

	

Product Certification下至少有一个认证项（含Others）




Recyclable Material Management

	

This supplier already has material recycling management system certification qualifications, including:【贵司获得的可回收材料管理体系认证】

	

Certification of recyclable material management system下至少有一个认证项




Socially Responsibility Certification

	

The supplier has Social Responsibility Certification, including:【贵司通过的社会责任认证】

	

Social responsibility certification下至少有一个认证项




Raw Materials Reports

	

The supplier has Raw Materials reports, including:【贵司获得的原材料检测报告】

	

Test Reports for Raw Materials下至少有一个报告项（含Others）




其它（Others）

	

Environmental Protection

	

The supplier has relevant environmental protection qualifications, including:【贵司所具备的环保资质及能力】

	

1、Does the company have a factory environmental impact assessment report?
2、Does the company have an EIA reply?
3、Is the actual founding consistent with EIA acceptance comments?
4、Does the company have a Pollution discharge permit?
5、Does the company have Fixed pollution source pollution discharge registration?
以上5项至少有一项值为Yes








Digital Management Enterprise

	


The supplier enhances business efficiency through digitalization, which involves:【贵司所具备的数字化管理能力】

	

1、Has the enterprise started the construction of enterprise digitalization process at this stage?
2、Has the enterprise established a technical transformation plan?
3、Does the enterprise currently establish a database based on digital construction to effectively collect and monitor big data in real time?
4、Does the enterprise establish and use ERP system to realize digital management of production, procurement, quality control, logistics and other processes?
5、Does the enterprise establish and use OA system to manage all processes?
6、Has the enterprise established a CRM system for customer relationship management?
7、Has the enterprise established SAP system for the management of internal financial system?
8、Has the enterprise established an AI model to process big data and achieve decision enabling management?
以上8个问题有至少3项的值为Yes











Standardized Supplier Management

	

The company established and implemented an effective suppliers’ assessment procedure and corrective & preventive actions mechanism

	

1、Does the company establish and implement an effective suppliers’ assessment procedure?选：
Have the written procedures and followed records
2、Are corrective & preventive actions mechanism established and implemented effectively (including the suppliers / sub-contractors control, incoming inspection, process control, final inspection and customer complaint)?选：
Has the clear procedure and followed records
3、Is there a clear procedure for handling customer complaints?选：
Has the clear procedure and followed records
以上三项全部满足
